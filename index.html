Door supervision

Licensed door supervisors for bars, clubs, venues and private functions, managing entry, crowd safety and conflict calmly.

Close protection

From first call to officer on site.

A clear, short process so you know what happens next and when.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Call or send the enquiry form. We'll ask about your site, hours and concerns.

  2. Site assessment

    We review the risks, in person or remotely, and agree what good cover looks like.

  3. Your security plan

    You receive a written proposal with staffing, duties and a fixed price.

  4. Deployment and review

    Briefed officers start on the agreed date, and we review performance with you regularly.

The standards behind every shift.

You should know exactly who is protecting your premises and how they were chosen.

Licensed

Every front-line officer holds a valid Security Industry Authority licence for their role.

Vetted

Staff are screened in line with BS 7858, including identity, right-to-work and employment history checks.

First-aid trained

Officers are trained in emergency first aid so they can respond while help is on the way.

Fully insured

Public and employer's liability insurance is in place for every assignment we take on.
